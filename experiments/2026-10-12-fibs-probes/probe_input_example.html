Carrot-Parsnip is a social deduction game. Players are secretly assigned roles:
- 2 players are Carrots
- 1 player is the Parsnip

The game consists of a single elimination vote. All players vote for one player
to eliminate. Any player receiving 2 or more votes is eliminated. Before the vote there will be 3 rounds of conversation where players are selected to speak in a random order each round. This means that a player who has not spoken in the early game may simply have not been selected to speak yet.

WINNING CONDITIONS:
